# Qualitative material

The authors examined 12 discovery pairs (D01–D12), then annotated 12 separate
verification pairs (V01–V12). These files contain the supplied codebook, full
response pairs and recorded author notes. Counts summarize selected cases, not
population rates or measured harm.

- [Codebook](codebook.md): theme claims, inclusion/exclusion rules, boundary examples and discovery anchors, in the source wording.
- [Discovery](discovery.md): D01–D12, both answers and author memos.
- [Verification](verification.md): V01–V12, both answers, statuses, directions, quotations, reasons and alternative readings.
- [Verification matrix](verification_matrix.csv): all 48 recorded statuses.
- [Case sources](case_sources.json): model, query, condition and phrasing identifiers for the 24 pairs.

## Theme correspondence

The supplied workbook uses `T01a`, `T01b`, `T02`, `T03`; the paper uses `T01`–`T04`.
The source identifiers are retained in these files.

| Paper theme | Workbook code | Recorded present cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T01 Synthetic Solidarity | T01a | V01, V06, V08, V09, V11 (5) |
| T02 Reduced agency & rescuing framing | T01b, narrower rescuing/infantilizing code | V01, V06, V08, V09 (4) |
| T03 Practical Advice Displacement or Lowered Expectations | T02 | V01, V02, V03, V05, V06, V07, V08, V09, V11, V12 (10) |
| T04 Causal Attribution of Disclosure to Query | T03 | V01, V03, V05, V08, V09, V11, V12 (7); V06 unclear |

The paper's broader reduced-agency/rescuing grouping lists V01, V03, V06, V08,
V09, V11 and V12 (7). The workbook separates rescuing/infantilizing framing
from lowered task expectations, placing the latter under practical displacement.
In the workbook, V03, V11 and V12 are absent under T01b and present under T02.
The matrix preserves those judgments; it does not reproduce a seven-case
rescuing count. The paired notes are supplied for inspection of both groupings.

## Transcription

Response fragments split across spreadsheet cells are joined in their original
order. Nonblank annotations, alternative readings and source-completeness notes
are retained. Blank fields, time logs, workbook instructions, macros and document
metadata are omitted. The source theme wording is retained, including its
proposed fixed-length explanation for displacement; this explanation is not
tested by the qualitative status counts. Original workbook hashes are recorded
in `data/source_hashes.json`.
